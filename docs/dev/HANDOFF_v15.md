# HANDOFF v15 — an expansion delivers

design:D-02, via #109. A land strip arrives with its sub-belt and sells its
machines: a dropper on its own line and a refiner that levels every dropper
on the plot (`Config.incomeRate` multiplies globally, so the refiner is #93's
"one upgrade level per dropper" made literal). The rows sit on the land
tracks after their strip, so the chain enforces the issue's strict order —
ground, its machines, more ground — and cross-side alternation stays
pricing's job.

The curve absorbed twenty income rungs and holds every band: 51-minute
build, first rebirth at minute 42 with eight rungs unbought, side tracks at
33%. The top half of the spine stretched (×1.3 exponent on a 100k anchor);
BaseCost derives to 9.1e9 now, from `upgrader6` at rank 6. #90 re-prices all
of this against a seven-day arc — the bands it must then hold are its own.

## 1. What only Studio can tell you

1. **A strip's line, running.** Buy landL1 then its dropper: the sub-belt
   surfaces and plain collector stand inside `Land_landL1`, drops ride
   frontward and retire at the collector. Nobody has seen an eleven-belt
   plot.
2. **The refiner's flash on a strip.** The arch stands outboard at the
   narrowest lots (19 wide, machine face 18 from the inner edge) — confirm
   it clears the moved side wall by eye, and that the flash reads.
3. **Machine pedestals on the aisle side.** A strip's buy pads sit inboard
   of its belt, one stud onto the neighbouring ground by design. Walkable?
4. **Teardown mid-ride.** Release with strip drops in flight: the strip
   model dies with its belt; the pooled bodies must not leak (clearDrops
   covers the counter; the variant leak is the sharp edge).
5. **Rebirth on a grown plot.** The ground stays, the strip machines
   vanish, their pads relight, and the strip belts keep standing (they are
   the strip's, land-owned) with nothing riding them until re-bought.
6. **Drop density at 80.** The budget rose from 70 for eleven belts;
   whether ~800 cosmetic drops server-wide is a frame cost is a real-server
   question.

## 2. Seams left deliberately open

- **#125 collapses the five-plus-two tracks into three categories.** The
  land tracks' three-kinds-per-strip layout is the shape it inherits; the
  strict chain is what "conveyor upgrades" becomes.
- **#98's storage cap** gets its "capacity grows with the plot" from
  `landCounts` — the growth axis now has income attached, so the cap curve
  has something to follow.
- **#90 re-prices everything.** The stretch exponent was chosen to hold
  today's 45–60 band; the seven-day retune replaces it and must model
  sitting → gap → sitting.
